# Static GK — The Shortlist Worth Memorising

> Static GK has low marginal returns. Learn this list, then stop. Spend the time you save on securities market awareness, which is worth far more marks.

| Institution | HQ | Note |

| **IOSCO** | Madrid | **International Organization of Securities Commissions — SEBI is a member; know this one** |

| ADB | Manila | Asian Development Bank |
| AIIB | Beijing | NDB (BRICS bank) is at **Shanghai** |

## Indices and reports (who publishes what)

- **Ease of Doing Business** — discontinued by the World Bank; replaced by **B-READY**.
- **Global Innovation Index** — WIPO. **Human Development Index** — UNDP.
- **World Economic Outlook** — IMF. **Global Financial Stability Report** — IMF.
- **Financial Stability Report** — **RBI**. **Trend and Progress of Banking in India** — RBI.
- **Economic Survey** — Ministry of Finance (CEA).
- **Global Competitiveness Report** — WEF. **Corruption Perceptions Index** — Transparency International.

## Exam pointers

1. **IOSCO — Madrid**. SEBI is a member and signed the **IOSCO MMoU**; expect a question.
2. **AIIB Beijing, NDB Shanghai** — routinely swapped in options.
3. **Financial Stability Report = RBI**; **Global Financial Stability Report = IMF**.
4. **Kotak Committee = corporate governance**; **Bimal Jalan = MII ownership**; **L C Gupta = derivatives**.
5. SIDBI is at **Lucknow** — the one institution that is not in Mumbai or Delhi.
