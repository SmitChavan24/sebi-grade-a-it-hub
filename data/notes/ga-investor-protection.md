# Investor Protection, Grievance Redress and ODR

## 1. Why this is SEBI's first mandate

The preamble puts **protection of investors** before development and regulation. Every mechanism below exists because retail investors are structurally disadvantaged in information and bargaining power.

## 2. SCORES — SEBI Complaints Redress System

- A **web-based centralised grievance portal**; complaints are lodged online and routed to the entity concerned, with defined turnaround times and escalation.
- **SCORES 2.0** (launched 2024) added **auto-routing, auto-escalation, monitoring by designated bodies** and reduced timelines. Complaints can also be filed through the **SEBI SCORES mobile app**.
- Flow: investor first approaches the entity → if unresolved, lodges on SCORES → the entity responds through the portal → review/escalation → if still unresolved, the **ODR** route.

## 3. ODR — Online Dispute Resolution (SMART ODR)

SEBI operationalised a **common ODR portal** for the securities market (2023). It provides **conciliation followed by arbitration**, conducted online through empanelled ODR institutions, with timelines and cost caps. It sits **after** the direct-with-entity and SCORES steps.

## 4. Funds that protect investors

- **IPEF — Investor Protection and Education Fund** (SEBI): funded by penalties, disgorgement, unclaimed amounts, grants; used for investor education and, in specified circumstances, restitution.
- **IPF at exchanges and depositories** — compensates investors for a **defaulting member** up to a specified limit.
- **Core SGF** at clearing corporations — protects the settlement system, not individual investors.
- **IEPF** (Ministry of Corporate Affairs, under the Companies Act) — unclaimed dividends and shares; a different fund from SEBI's IPEF. Do not confuse them.

## 5. Key protective mechanisms

- **KYC through KRAs**, **PAN as the unique identifier**, **nomination mandatory or explicit opt-out**.
- **Segregation of client funds and securities**; **upstreaming** of client funds to clearing corporations; **client-level margin reporting**.
- **Running account settlement** — brokers must settle client funds periodically.
- **Direct payout of securities** to the client demat account.
- **Investor charter** for every intermediary category, disclosing rights, services and timelines.
- **Risk disclosure** requirements, including for equity F&O.
- **Unified KYC (CKYC)**, **e-KYC**, and the **KYC Registration Agencies** regime.
- **Investor awareness programmes**, **SEBI Investor website (investor.sebi.gov.in)**, and the **"Saa₹thi" investor education mobile app**.

## 6. Frauds investors must be warned about

Ponzi and multi-level marketing schemes disguised as investments; **unregistered advisers** promising assured returns; **dabba trading** (off-exchange trading); social-media "tips" groups and **front-running of recommendations**; **deepfake and impersonation scams** using the names of genuine market participants. SEBI's standard advice: **check the registration number**, and remember that **no registered entity may promise assured returns**.

---

## Exam pointers

1. **IPEF is SEBI's; IEPF is MCA's.** They are different funds.
2. Order of escalation: **entity → SCORES → ODR (conciliation then arbitration)**.
3. **IPF** at exchanges compensates for **member default**, not for market losses.
4. Assured-return promises are the single clearest marker of an illegal scheme.
5. SCORES 2.0 and the SMART ODR portal are recent — quote them by name.
