# RBI, Monetary Policy and the Banking System

## 1. RBI basics

- Established **1 April 1935** under the RBI Act, 1934, on the recommendation of the **Hilton Young Commission**; **nationalised 1 January 1949**. Headquarters: **Mumbai** (moved from Kolkata in 1937).
- **Central Board**: Governor + up to 4 Deputy Governors + government-nominated directors.
- Functions: note issue (**Minimum Reserve System** since 1957 — ₹200 crore of assets, of which ₹115 crore in gold), banker to the government, banker to banks, regulator and supervisor, manager of forex (FEMA), manager of the public debt, developmental roles, and operator of payment systems.

## 2. Monetary policy framework

- **Flexible Inflation Targeting** since 2016 (amendment to the RBI Act). Target: **CPI inflation of 4%, with a band of +/- 2%**.
- **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** — **six members**: 3 from RBI (Governor as chair, a Deputy Governor, one officer) and **3 external members** appointed by the government for 4 years. Decisions by majority; the **Governor has a casting vote**. Meets at least **four times a year** (in practice, six bi-monthly meetings).
- **Failure** to maintain the target for **three consecutive quarters** requires a report to the government.

## 3. Policy instruments

| Instrument | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Repo rate** | Rate at which RBI lends to banks against securities |
| **Reverse repo** | Rate at which RBI absorbs liquidity (largely replaced by SDF) |
| **SDF** | Standing Deposit Facility — absorbs liquidity **without collateral** (introduced 2022) |
| **MSF** | Marginal Standing Facility — emergency borrowing, above the repo rate |
| **Bank rate** | Long-term rate; aligned with MSF; penal rate for CRR/SLR shortfalls |
| **CRR** | Cash Reserve Ratio — % of NDTL as cash with RBI; **no interest paid** |
| **SLR** | Statutory Liquidity Ratio — % of NDTL in gold, cash or approved securities |
| **OMO** | Open market operations — purchase/sale of government securities |
| **LAF corridor** | MSF (ceiling) — repo (policy rate) — SDF (floor) |

Qualitative tools: margin requirements, moral suasion, direct action, selective credit control.

## 4. Banking terms you must know

- **NPA** — an asset overdue for **more than 90 days**. Classification: Standard → **Sub-standard** (up to 12 months as NPA) → **Doubtful** → **Loss**. **SMA-0/1/2** are early warning categories before NPA.
- **Provisioning Coverage Ratio**, **CRAR / Capital Adequacy** under **Basel III** (minimum 9% in India plus CCB), **Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital**, **leverage ratio**, **LCR** and **NSFR**.
- **PCA — Prompt Corrective Action**: triggered by breaches of capital, asset quality (net NPA) and leverage thresholds.
- **SARFAESI Act, 2002** — enforcement of security interest without court intervention; **ARCs**; **DRT**; and the **IBC, 2016** for resolution (270/330-day timelines).
- **Priority Sector Lending** — 40% of ANBC for commercial banks, with sub-targets for agriculture (18%), weaker sections (12%) and micro enterprises (7.5%). **PSLCs** allow trading of the obligation.
- **Deposit insurance** by **DICGC** — up to **₹5 lakh** per depositor per bank (raised from ₹1 lakh in 2020).

## 5. Payment systems (directly relevant to IT)

**NPCI** (2008, a not-for-profit under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007) runs **UPI, IMPS, NACH, AePS, RuPay, NETC FASTag, BBPS, NFS ATM network**. RTGS (real time, gross, high value, **24x7 since December 2020**), NEFT (half-hourly batches, **24x7 since December 2019**). **CBDC (e-Rupee)** pilots in retail and wholesale since 2022. **Account Aggregator** framework for consented financial data sharing; **OCEN** for credit rails.

## Exam pointers

1. **CPI target 4% +/- 2%**; MPC has **6 members**, Governor has the **casting vote**.
2. **SDF is collateral-free** and forms the floor of the LAF corridor.
3. NPA at **90 days**; DICGC insurance **₹5 lakh**.
4. CRR earns **no interest**; SLR is held in approved securities and does earn returns.
5. NPCI operates UPI; **RBI** regulates payment systems under the **PSS Act, 2007**.
