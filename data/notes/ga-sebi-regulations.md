# The SEBI Regulations You Must Know By Name

> You will not be asked to recite regulations, but you will be expected to know which one governs what. Learn the five-letter shortlist perfectly; recognise the rest.

## 1. LODR — Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015

The corporate governance and disclosure rulebook for listed companies.

- **Regulation 17** — Board of Directors: at least **6 directors**; at least **one woman director** (and an **independent woman director** for the top 1000 listed entities by market cap); at least **one-third independent directors**, and **half** if the chairperson is an executive or a promoter.
- **Regulation 18** — **Audit Committee**: minimum 3 directors, **two-thirds independent**, all financially literate; chaired by an independent director.
- **Regulation 19** — Nomination and Remuneration Committee. **Regulation 20** — Stakeholders Relationship Committee. **Regulation 21** — Risk Management Committee (top 1000 entities).
- **Regulation 23** — **Related Party Transactions**: prior approval of the audit committee; material RPTs need shareholder approval, and **related parties cannot vote**.
- **Regulation 24A** — annual **secretarial audit**.
- **Regulation 30** — **disclosure of material events**, with a **quantitative materiality threshold** and strict timelines (12/24 hours depending on the event); the 2023 amendments tightened this considerably and added **verification of market rumours** for large listed entities.
- **Regulation 33** — quarterly **financial results**. **Regulation 31** — shareholding pattern. **Regulation 34** — annual report, including the **Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR)** for the top listed entities, with **BRSR Core** assurance.
- **Regulation 44** — **e-voting** for shareholder resolutions.

## 2. ICDR — Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2018

Governs public issues, rights issues, preferential allotment, QIP, bonus, IPO eligibility (the profitability route vs the 75%-to-QIB route), promoter contribution (**20% minimum, locked in**), pricing formulas and disclosure standards in the offer document.

## 3. PIT — Prohibition of Insider Trading Regulations, 2015

- **UPSI** — Unpublished Price Sensitive Information: information not generally available which, on becoming available, is likely to **materially affect the price** of securities. Illustrative list: financial results, dividends, change in capital structure, mergers/acquisitions/delisting, changes in key managerial personnel.
- **Insider** — a **connected person**, or anyone **in possession of or having access to UPSI**. Note that possession is enough; you need not be an employee.
- **Trading Plans**, **Code of Fair Disclosure**, **Structured Digital Database (SDD)** — every listed entity must maintain a database of persons with whom UPSI is shared, with time stamps and **non-tamperable audit trails**. *This is an IT-relevant requirement: know it.*
- **Contra-trade restriction** (six months), **trading window closure**, and **legitimate purpose** exceptions.

## 4. SAST — Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers Regulations, 2011

- **25% trigger** — acquiring 25% or more of voting rights requires an **open offer**.
- **Creeping acquisition** — a holder between 25% and the maximum permissible limit may acquire up to **5% in a financial year** without an open offer.
- **Open offer size:** minimum **26%** of the target's shares.
- **Indirect acquisition, competing offers, exemptions** (inter-se promoter transfers, rights issues) and the role of the **manager to the offer**.

## 5. PFUTP — Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices Regulations, 2003

Prohibits manipulation, misleading statements, and market abuse. Classic patterns: **circular trading, synchronised trades, pump and dump, front running, spoofing/layering, wash trades, misleading corporate announcements**. Penalty under **S.15HA** of the SEBI Act.

**SECC Regulations** matter most for the IT stream: they carry the governance, **technology, system audit, business continuity and cyber requirements** for exchanges and clearing corporations.

## Exam pointers

1. **LODR 17/18/23/30/33** are the regulation numbers most often asked.
2. PIT's **Structured Digital Database** is a technology obligation — a natural IT-stream interview question.
3. SAST: **25% trigger, 5% creeping, 26% open offer**.
4. PFUTP → penalty under **S.15HA**; insider trading → **S.15G**.
5. **SECC 2018** governs MIIs — know it by name.
