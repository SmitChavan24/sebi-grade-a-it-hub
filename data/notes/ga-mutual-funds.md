# Mutual Funds, AIFs and Portfolio Management

## 1. Structure of an Indian mutual fund (three-tier)

```
Sponsor  (sets up the fund; min 40% of AMC net worth; track record of 5 years)
   |
Trust / Trustees  (hold assets for unit holders; at least 2/3 independent)
   |
AMC  (Asset Management Company — manages schemes; min net worth Rs 50 crore)
   +-- Custodian (holds securities, SEBI-registered, independent of sponsor)
   +-- RTA (registrar and transfer agent)
```

Governed by the **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996**. Industry body: **AMFI** (an SRO-like association, not a statutory regulator).

## 2. Key concepts

- **NAV** = (Assets − Liabilities) ÷ Units outstanding. Computed daily for open-ended schemes.
- **Open-ended** — continuous subscription/redemption at NAV. **Close-ended** — fixed tenure, listed on an exchange. **Interval** — hybrid.
- **TER — Total Expense Ratio**: capped on a slab basis by AUM; **direct plans** have a lower TER than **regular plans** because they carry no distributor commission.
- **Exit load**, **lock-in** (ELSS: 3 years), **SIP / SWP / STP**.
- **Riskometer** — six levels from Low to Very High; scheme risk must be disclosed and updated monthly.
- **Scheme categorisation (2017-18):** SEBI standardised categories so that one AMC can have only one scheme per category — Equity (11 categories including large cap, mid cap, small cap, flexi cap, ELSS), Debt (16), Hybrid (6), Solution-oriented (2), Other (index funds, FoF).

## 3. Types worth knowing

- **Index funds and ETFs** — passive, track an index; ETFs trade on the exchange and need a demat account. **Tracking error** is the key quality metric.
- **Liquid, overnight, money market, ultra-short, corporate bond, credit risk, gilt** — debt categories by maturity/credit.
- **Fund of Funds**, **International funds**, **Gold/Silver ETFs**, **Target maturity funds**.

## 4. Alternative Investment Funds (AIF) — Regulations, 2012

| Category | What it invests in | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Category I** | Socially/economically desirable sectors | Venture capital, SME, infrastructure, social venture, angel funds |
| **Category II** | Funds that are neither I nor III and do not use leverage except for operations | Private equity, debt funds, real estate funds |
| **Category III** | Complex/diverse strategies, may use leverage | Hedge funds, PIPE funds |

- Minimum investment by an investor: **₹1 crore** (₹25 lakh for employees/directors of the manager).
- Minimum corpus: **₹20 crore** (₹10 crore for angel funds).
- Maximum **1,000 investors** per scheme (angel funds: 200 in earlier norms — verify).

## 5. Portfolio Management Services (PMS)

- Minimum investment **₹50 lakh** (raised from ₹25 lakh in 2020).
- **Discretionary** (manager decides), **non-discretionary** (client decides), **advisory**.
- Securities are held in the client's own demat account — a key difference from mutual funds.

## 6. Investment advisers vs research analysts vs distributors

| | Investment Adviser | Research Analyst | MF Distributor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regulation | IA Regulations, 2013 | RA Regulations, 2014 | AMFI registration (ARN) |
| Earns from | Fee from the client | Research subscription/employer | Commission from the AMC |
| Duty | **Fiduciary**, must act in the client's best interest | Disclosure of conflicts | Suitability, no advice for a fee |

The separation of **advice** from **distribution** — you cannot charge a fee for advice and also earn a commission on the same client — is a core SEBI principle.

---

## Exam pointers

1. Three-tier: **Sponsor → Trustee → AMC**; the **trust** structure is what protects unit holders.
2. AMC minimum net worth **₹50 crore**; sponsor must contribute at least **40%** of it.
3. **AIF: Cat I socially desirable, Cat II PE/debt, Cat III hedge-like with leverage.** Minimum ticket **₹1 crore**.
4. **PMS minimum ₹50 lakh**; securities held in the client's own name.
5. ELSS lock-in **3 years**; direct plans always have a lower TER than regular plans.
