# Previous Year Questions — What Exists, What Doesn't

> Read this before you spend money or hours hunting for "SEBI Grade A IT previous year papers PDF". Knowing what genuinely exists will save you both.

## 1. The honest position

**SEBI does not publish its question papers.** There is no official PYQ PDF, no official answer key, and nothing on sebi.gov.in beyond the notification, the syllabus outline and the results. This is normal for regulator recruitment in India — RBI Grade A, NABARD and IRDAI behave the same way.

What circulates instead:

| Source | What it actually is | How much to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| Coaching "PYQ" PDFs and videos | **Memory-based** reconstructions by candidates after the exam | Useful for *pattern and topic weight*. Individual questions and options are often wrong or garbled |
| YouTube "exam analysis" videos, posted within a day of the exam | Candidates recalling what appeared, section by section | The most reliable signal on **difficulty and topic mix** |
| Telegram/forum dumps claiming "original paper" | Almost always recycled from other exams or fabricated | Ignore |
| GATE / UGC-NET / ISRO / bank IT officer papers | **Genuine, official, freely available** past papers | High — and the IT syllabus overlaps heavily |

**The practical conclusion:** the closest thing to real practice on genuine past questions is **GATE CS and UGC-NET Computer Science papers**, which are official, free, and cover OS, DBMS, CN, DSA, COA and programming to a *higher* standard than SEBI needs. Use those for the IT paper, and use coaching memory-based material only to calibrate what to expect.

## 2. What candidates consistently report about the IT paper

Treat this as a working model, not gospel — verify against the analysis videos for your own attempt.

**Phase I Paper 2 (IT), 100 questions in 40 minutes.** That is roughly **24 seconds per question**. This single number tells you everything about the paper's character:

- Questions are **short, factual and recall-based**. There is no time for long numericals.
- Expect definitions, comparisons ("which of the following is NOT…"), protocol/port matching, one-line concept checks, small output-prediction snippets.
- Speed and coverage beat depth. A candidate who knows 80% of the syllabus shallowly will outscore one who knows 40% deeply.

**Topic mix that is repeatedly reported** (approximate, and it does shift):

| Area | Rough share | Character |
|---|---|---|
| DBMS & SQL | high | Normalisation, keys, joins, transactions |
| Computer Networks | high | Layers, protocols, ports, IP/subnetting |
| Operating Systems | high | Scheduling, deadlock, memory, paging |
| Programming & OOP | high | Output questions, OOP concepts, C/Java basics |
| Data Structures & Algorithms | medium | Complexity, properties, traversals |
| Cyber Security | medium-high, rising | Attacks, cryptography, IT Act, SEBI cyber framework |
| Cloud, Big Data, AI, Blockchain | medium, rising | Definitions and current-technology awareness |
| Computer Organisation | low-medium | Number systems, cache, pipelining basics |
| Software Engineering | low-medium | SDLC, testing, models |
| IT in securities markets | low but distinctive | Where SEBI-specific questions appear |

**Phase II Paper 2 (IT)** goes deeper and adds a descriptive component — design questions, short essays on technology in markets, SQL or code writing. This is where depth finally pays.

**Phase I Paper 1** follows the standard bank/regulator pattern: General Awareness (with a strong securities-market tilt), English, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning. Anyone who has prepared for IBPS/SBI PO will recognise it.

## 3. Where to find genuine, official past papers (free)

- **GATE Computer Science (CS/IT)** — official past papers and keys published by the organising IIT each year. Best possible practice for OS, DBMS, CN, DSA, COA. Search "GATE CS previous year papers official".
- **UGC-NET Computer Science & Applications** — NTA publishes papers and answer keys. Closer to SEBI's recall-based style than GATE.
- **IBPS / SBI Specialist Officer (IT)** — official practice material, closest in format to a regulator IT paper.
- **NIELIT, ISRO scientist/engineer CS papers** — official, free, good for COA and networks.
- For **Paper 1**, any free bank PO question set is directly transferable.

## 4. How to use PYQ material properly

1. **Do not chase authenticity you cannot verify.** A memory-based question with a wrong option teaches you a wrong fact. If a question's answer contradicts your notes, trust the notes and the bare Act.
2. **Mine them for pattern, not content** — which topics, what depth, what phrasing.
3. **Watch one exam-analysis video** per past attempt, take notes on topic weights, then close YouTube. That is the whole value.
4. **Replace hunting with practising.** The Question Bank in this app, the pattern-accurate mock papers, and GATE/NET papers will do more for your score than a perfect reconstruction of last year's paper ever could.
5. **For anything regulatory** — SEBI Act sections, penalties, CSCRF details — coaching PYQ answers age badly. Verify on sebi.gov.in.

## Exam pointers

1. **SEBI publishes no official past papers.** Anything claiming to be "the original paper" is memory-based at best.
2. **24 seconds per question** in Phase I Paper 2 — the paper rewards breadth and speed, not depth.
3. Use **GATE CS and UGC-NET CS** for genuine, official, free past questions on the IT syllabus.
4. Memory-based papers are for **topic weights**, never for learning facts.
5. When a PYQ answer and your notes disagree on regulation, **the bare Act wins**.
