# SEBI — Establishment, Structure, Powers and Functions

> Assume you will be asked "What does SEBI do?" in the interview. A generic answer is a wasted opportunity. Answer with the **three-fold preamble**, then give one live example of each mandate.

## 1. Establishment

- Set up as a **non-statutory body on 12 April 1988** by a government resolution.
- Given **statutory status by the SEBI Act, 1992**, brought into force from **30 January 1992** (ordinance route; the Act received assent on 4 April 1992 with retrospective effect).
- Trigger: the 1991–92 securities scam and the repeal of the **Capital Issues (Control) Act, 1947**, which had made the Controller of Capital Issues the pricing authority. With the CCI gone, issuers got **free pricing** — and the market needed a disclosure-based regulator.
- **Headquarters:** Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai. **Regional offices:** Northern (New Delhi), Eastern (Kolkata), Southern (Chennai), Western (Ahmedabad), plus local offices.

## 2. The preamble — memorise it

> "…to protect the interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate, the securities market and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto."

Three mandates, deliberately in tension:

| Mandate | Example |
|---|---|
| **Protection** | Grievance redress (SCORES), disclosure norms, action on mis-selling |
| **Development** | REITs/InvITs, T+1 settlement, UPI in IPOs, Social Stock Exchange |
| **Regulation** | Registration, inspection, investigation, penalties, debarment |

## 3. Composition of the Board (Section 4)

1. A **Chairman**;
2. **Two members** from the Ministries dealing with **Finance** and the administration of the **Companies Act**;
3. **One member** nominated by the **RBI**;
4. **Five other members**, of whom **at least three are whole-time members**.

Total strength **nine**; appointments are made by the **Central Government**.

## 4. Functions (Section 11)

Section 11(1) states the duty; **Section 11(2)** lists the measures:

- regulating the **business in stock exchanges** and any securities market;
- **registering and regulating intermediaries** — brokers, share transfer agents, bankers to an issue, trustees of trust deeds, registrars, merchant bankers, underwriters, portfolio managers, investment advisers, depositories, custodians, FPIs, credit rating agencies;
- registering and regulating **collective investment schemes** including **mutual funds**;
- promoting and regulating **self-regulatory organisations**;
- **prohibiting fraudulent and unfair trade practices**;
- **prohibiting insider trading**;
- regulating **substantial acquisition of shares and takeovers**;
- promoting **investor education** and training of intermediaries;
- **inspection, inquiries and audits** of exchanges, intermediaries and SROs;
- calling for information from banks and authorities;
- levying **fees and other charges**;
- conducting **research**.

## 5. Powers — the three hats

**(a) Quasi-legislative** — it *makes* rules. SEBI frames regulations under **Section 30** (LODR, ICDR, PIT, SAST, PFUTP, Mutual Funds, AIF and so on). Regulations are laid before Parliament.

**(b) Quasi-executive** — it *enforces*: registration (S.12), inspection and **investigation (S.11C)**, search and seizure, **directions (S.11B)** including **disgorgement**, and civil-court powers under **S.11(3)**.

**(c) Quasi-judicial** — it *adjudicates*: Adjudicating Officers under **S.15-I** impose monetary penalties; the Board debars persons from the market. Appeals lie to **SAT**.

Because SEBI wears all three hats, its orders are appealable and its regulations reviewable — a standard discussion question.

## 6. Key departments (useful in the interview)

Market Regulation (MRD) · Market Intermediaries Regulation and Supervision (MIRSD) · Corporation Finance (CFD) · Investment Management (IMD — MFs, AIFs, REITs) · Integrated Surveillance (ISD) · Enforcement · Legal Affairs · Investigations · **Information Technology (ITD)** · Economic and Policy Analysis (DEPA) · Office of Investor Assistance and Education (OIAE).

> **If asked which department you would want:** the Information Technology Department — and have reasons ready: MII system audits, cyber-resilience supervision under CSCRF, surveillance analytics, and SEBI's own digital infrastructure.

## 7. Accountability

- SEBI submits an **annual report** to the Central Government, laid before **both Houses of Parliament**.
- Accounts are audited by the **CAG**.
- The Central Government may issue **written policy directions (S.16)** and may **supersede the Board (S.17)**.
- SEBI is funded by **fees and charges** from market participants, not by a budgetary grant.

## Exam pointers

1. 1988 = set up; 1992 = statutory.
2. Nine members; **at least three whole-time** among the "five other members".
3. S.11 functions · S.11B directions · S.11C investigation · S.12 registration · S.15-I adjudication · S.15T appeal · S.30 regulations.
4. SEBI regulates **securities markets** — not banks, insurance or pensions.
5. The preamble's word order — *protect, develop, regulate* — is itself a question.
