# Market Instruments — Equity, Debt and Hybrids

## 1. Equity instruments

- **Equity shares** — ownership, residual claim, voting rights, dividend not guaranteed.
- **Preference shares** — fixed dividend, priority over equity in dividend and winding up, usually no voting rights. Types: cumulative/non-cumulative, participating/non-participating, convertible/non-convertible, redeemable (must be redeemed within **20 years** under the Companies Act; 30 years for infrastructure projects).
- **DVR — Differential Voting Rights** shares; SEBI permits **superior voting rights (SR) shares** for founders of tech companies subject to strict conditions and sunset clauses.
- **Sweat equity, ESOP, warrants.**
- **Depository receipts:** **ADR** (US), **GDR** (other markets), **IDR** (foreign company raising in India). The underlying shares are held by a custodian; the receipt trades abroad.

## 2. Debt instruments

| Instrument | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Debenture** | Corporate debt; secured or unsecured; NCDs are the listed form |
| **Bond** | Generic debt security, usually with a coupon |
| **G-sec** | Sovereign, issued by RBI on behalf of the government; dated securities and T-bills |
| **SDL** | State Development Loans |
| **Masala bond** | Rupee-denominated bond issued **outside India** |
| **Municipal bond** | Issued by urban local bodies; SEBI framework |
| **Green / ESG bond** | Proceeds earmarked for environmental projects |
| **Perpetual / AT1 bond** | No maturity; issued by banks for Basel III capital; can be written down |

**Bond mathematics you must know:**
- Price and yield move in **opposite** directions.
- **Current yield** = annual coupon ÷ market price. **YTM** = the discount rate equating price to the present value of all cash flows.
- **Duration** measures interest-rate sensitivity; **modified duration** estimates the % price change for a 1% yield change. **Convexity** corrects duration for large moves.
- **Credit rating** scale: AAA (highest safety) down through AA, A, BBB (lowest investment grade), BB and below (speculative), D (default).

## 3. Hybrid and structured instruments

- **Convertible debentures** — FCD (fully), PCD (partly), OCD (optionally).
- **REITs** — Real Estate Investment Trusts. Must distribute at least **90% of net distributable cash flows**; at least **80% of assets in completed, income-generating property**. **SM REITs** (small and medium REITs) were introduced by SEBI in 2024.
- **InvITs** — Infrastructure Investment Trusts; similar structure for infrastructure assets.
- **Securitised debt instruments / pass-through certificates**; **security receipts** under SARFAESI.
- **Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP)** instruments on the **Social Stock Exchange**.

## 4. Structure of REIT/InvIT (frequently asked)

```
Sponsor  -> sets up and holds a minimum stake
Trustee  -> independent, holds assets for unit holders (SEBI-registered debenture trustee)
Manager  -> runs operations and investment decisions
Unit holders -> hold listed units, receive distributions
```

## Exam pointers

1. Masala bonds are **rupee-denominated but issued offshore** — currency risk sits with the investor.
2. AT1 bonds are **perpetual, loss-absorbing** and were at the centre of a well-known Indian banking episode — know the risk story.
3. REIT: **90% distribution, 80% completed assets**.
4. Price and yield move **inversely**; duration measures the sensitivity.
5. IDR = foreign company raising in India; ADR/GDR = Indian company raising abroad.
