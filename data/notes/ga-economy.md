# Indian Economy — The Part That Gets Asked

## 1. National income

- **GDP** = C + I + G + (X − M). **GVA** = GDP − product taxes + product subsidies. India's official releases use both.
- **Nominal vs real** GDP; the **GDP deflator** = nominal ÷ real × 100.
- **Base year** for the current GDP series: **2011-12** (a revision to a newer base year has been under discussion — verify the current position).
- **NSO / MoSPI** publishes GDP; advance, provisional and revised estimates.

## 2. Inflation

| Index | Published by | Base year | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CPI (Combined)** | NSO | 2012 | The **monetary policy target** index |
| **WPI** | Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT | 2011-12 | No services; wholesale transactions |

**Types:** demand-pull, cost-push, core (excluding food and fuel), headline, imported. **Phillips curve** — the short-run inflation-unemployment trade-off. **Stagflation** = high inflation with stagnant growth.

## 3. Fiscal side

- **Budget documents:** Annual Financial Statement (Art. 112), Demands for Grants, Finance Bill, **FRBM** statements, **Expenditure Budget**, **Receipt Budget**.
- **Fiscal deficit** = total expenditure − total receipts excluding borrowings. **Revenue deficit** = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts. **Primary deficit** = fiscal deficit − interest payments. **Effective revenue deficit** excludes grants for capital asset creation.
- **FRBM Act, 2003** — targets and the escape clause. **Finance Commission** (Art. 280) — the 15th FC recommended a **41% share** of the divisible pool for states.
- **GST** — Article 246A, **GST Council** (Art. 279A) with the Union having one-third weight and states two-thirds; slabs 0/5/12/18/28 plus cess. **Compensation cess**.
- Taxes: direct (income, corporate) vs indirect (GST, customs); **tax buoyancy**, **tax-to-GDP ratio**, **laffer curve**.

## 4. External sector

- **Balance of Payments** = **Current account** (trade + invisibles) + **Capital/Financial account** + errors and omissions + reserve movements.
- **CAD**, **FDI vs FPI**, **ECB**, **NRI deposits**, **forex reserves** (FCA + gold + SDR + reserve tranche position).
- **Exchange rate regimes**: India runs a **managed float**. **NEER/REER**. **Depreciation vs devaluation**.
- **FEMA, 1999** replaced FERA; **FDI policy** — automatic vs government route; sectoral caps.

## 5. Growth, poverty and employment

- **PLFS** for employment (unemployment rate, LFPR, WPR). **MPCE survey** for consumption.
- **HDI** (UNDP), **Multidimensional Poverty Index** (NITI Aayog's national MPI), **Gini coefficient**, **Lorenz curve**.
- **NITI Aayog** (2015, replaced the Planning Commission), **Economic Survey** (published before the Budget by the CEA).

## 6. Financial-sector economics worth a line in the interview

Financialisation of household savings (the shift from bank deposits and gold into mutual funds and equities), the rise of **SIP flows**, **retail participation** post-2020, **demat account growth**, and the policy question of **household leverage in derivatives**. These are the exact themes SEBI cares about, and they connect the economy section to your stream.

## Exam pointers

1. **CPI, not WPI**, is the monetary policy target.
2. Fiscal deficit excludes borrowings from receipts; **primary deficit = fiscal deficit − interest**.
3. GST Council: **Centre one-third, states two-thirds**; decisions need a **three-fourths majority**.
4. **Forex reserves** = FCA + gold + SDRs + reserve tranche position.
5. India runs a **managed float**, not a fixed or free float.
