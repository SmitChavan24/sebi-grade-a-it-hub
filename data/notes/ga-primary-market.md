# Primary Market — IPOs, Book Building, ASBA and UPI

> The primary market is where capital formation actually happens. Expect at least one question on the IPO process, and one on ASBA/UPI mechanics.

## 1. Ways a company raises capital

| Route | What it is |
|---|---|
| **IPO** | First sale of shares to the public; company becomes listed |
| **FPO** | Further public offer by an already-listed company |
| **Rights issue** | Shares offered to existing shareholders in a fixed ratio |
| **Bonus issue** | Free shares from reserves — no fresh capital raised |
| **Preferential allotment** | Shares to selected persons, priced per ICDR formula |
| **QIP** | Qualified Institutions Placement — quick route for listed companies to raise from QIBs |
| **Private placement** | Offer to fewer than 200 persons in a financial year (Companies Act) |
| **OFS** | Offer for Sale — promoters sell existing shares through the exchange mechanism |

In an IPO, a **fresh issue** brings new money into the company; an **offer for sale (OFS) component** only transfers money to selling shareholders. Know the difference — it is a favourite question.

## 2. The IPO process end to end

```
1. Board and shareholder approval
2. Appoint merchant banker (BRLM), registrar, legal counsel, auditors
3. Due diligence; file Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) with SEBI + exchanges
4. SEBI observations ("comments"); revise
5. File Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) with the RoC
6. Price band announced; anchor investor allocation (1 day before)
7. Issue opens: minimum 3 working days, maximum 10 working days
8. Bidding via ASBA (UPI for retail); basis of allotment finalised with the exchange
9. Allotment, refund/unblocking, credit to demat
10. Listing and trading
```

**Documents:** DRHP → RHP → Prospectus. A **red herring** prospectus omits the final price or number of shares.

## 3. Pricing methods

- **Fixed price issue** — price printed in the prospectus; demand known only after the issue closes; 50% of the issue reserved for applications up to ₹2 lakh.
- **Book building** — a price band (cap must not exceed 120% of the floor) within which investors bid; the **cut-off price** option is available to **retail individual investors** only. Demand is visible on the exchange website during the issue.

**Anchor investors:** QIBs who are allotted shares one day before the issue opens, with a lock-in (a portion for 30 days and a portion for 90 days under the revised norms). They signal quality and stabilise the book.

## 4. Investor categories and reservation (book-built issue)

| Category | Typical reservation |
|---|---|
| **QIB** (Qualified Institutional Buyer) | Not more than 50% |
| **NII / HNI** (Non-Institutional Investor, above ₹2 lakh) | Not less than 15% |
| **RII** (Retail Individual Investor, up to ₹2 lakh) | Not less than 35% |

If the issuer does not meet the profitability route under ICDR, at least **75% must be allotted to QIBs** — remember this contrast.

**Minimum subscription:** the issue must receive at least **90% subscription** of the fresh issue portion, failing which the money is refunded.

## 5. ASBA and UPI — the plumbing

**ASBA — Application Supported by Blocked Amount.** Your money stays in **your** bank account and is merely **blocked**; it is debited only on allotment. You keep earning interest, and refunds are instant unblocking rather than cheques. ASBA is **mandatory** for public issues.

**UPI mechanism for retail:** retail investors applying through an intermediary use their **UPI ID** as the payment instrument; the block happens through a UPI mandate. The limit for UPI-based IPO applications was raised to **₹5 lakh**. Verify the current cap.

Key participants: **SCSB** (Self Certified Syndicate Bank), **Sponsor Bank** (routes the UPI mandate), **Registrar (RTA)**.

## 6. Timelines

- **T+3 listing:** SEBI reduced the IPO listing timeline from T+6 to **T+3 working days** from the issue closing date — voluntary from September 2023 and mandatory for issues opening on or after 1 December 2023. Verify the current position.
- **Issue open period:** 3 to 10 working days.
- **Price band announcement:** at least 2 working days before opening.

## 7. Other primary-market instruments

- **SME platforms** — BSE SME and NSE Emerge, with lighter norms and a migration path to the main board.
- **Social Stock Exchange (SSE)** — a segment for not-for-profit organisations and social enterprises; **Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) instruments** are issued there.
- **REITs and InvITs** — trusts that hold income-generating real estate / infrastructure assets and list their units.
- **Green bonds and ESG debt** — SEBI framework for green debt securities.

---

## Exam pointers

1. **DRHP → RHP → Prospectus**; the red herring omits price or quantity.
2. **QIB ≤ 50%, NII ≥ 15%, RII ≥ 35%**; **75% to QIBs** if the issuer uses the non-profitability route.
3. **Cut-off price is only for retail** investors.
4. ASBA blocks funds in your own account; UPI is the retail payment rail.
5. **Minimum subscription 90%**; listing in **T+3** working days.
