# Other Regulators and Institutions

## IRDAI
- **Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India**, IRDA Act **1999**; headquarters **Hyderabad**.
- Regulates insurers, intermediaries, products and solvency. Key terms: **solvency margin (1.5x)**, **IRDAI Ombudsman**, **free-look period (15/30 days)**, **Bima Sugam / Bima Trinity** initiatives, **FDI in insurance up to 74%** (with a policy proposal to go higher — verify).

## PFRDA
- **Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority**; set up 2003, **statutory in 2013** (PFRDA Act).
- Regulates **NPS** (Tier I and Tier II accounts, PRAN, Point of Presence, Central Recordkeeping Agency — **Protean/NSDL e-Gov, KFintech, CAMS**), **APY** and the **NPS Trust**.

## IBBI
- **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India**, 2016, under the **IBC**. Regulates insolvency professionals, IPAs, information utilities. **CIRP** timeline 180 + 90 days, outer limit **330 days** including litigation. **Committee of Creditors**, **resolution professional**, **liquidation waterfall (S.53)**.

## IFSCA
- **International Financial Services Centres Authority**, 2020, **Gandhinagar**. A **unified regulator** for banking, capital markets, insurance and funds in **GIFT IFSC** — the only Indian regulator combining all four mandates. Know **NSE IFSC, India INX**, **bullion exchange (IIBX)**, and the aircraft/ship leasing and fund-management activities there.

## PMLA, 2002 — because every intermediary is a reporting entity
- Reporting entities must maintain records, do **KYC**, and report **STRs / CTRs** to **FIU-IND**.
- Record retention **five years**; **designated director** and **principal officer** must be appointed.
- SEBI issues its own **master circular on AML/CFT** for market intermediaries.

## Exam pointers

1. IRDAI **Hyderabad**, IFSCA **Gandhinagar**, IBBI and SEBI **Delhi/Mumbai** respectively — headquarters get asked.
2. IFSCA is the **only unified** financial regulator in India.
3. SAT hears appeals from **SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA and IBBI**.
4. CCIL clears **G-sec and forex**; clearing corporations of exchanges clear equities and derivatives.
5. PMLA reports go to **FIU-IND**, not to SEBI.
