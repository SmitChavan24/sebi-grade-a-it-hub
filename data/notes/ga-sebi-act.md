# SEBI Act, 1992 — Sections, Penalties and Appeals

> Learn the section numbers. In Phase I they are one-mark questions; in the interview, saying "Section 11B directions" instead of "SEBI told them to stop" changes how you are read.

## 1. Sections you must know cold

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **3** | Establishment of the Board |
| **4** | Management — Chairman + 8 members |
| **11** | Functions of the Board |
| **11(2A)** | Power to call for information and conduct inspections |
| **11(3)** | Civil court powers — discovery, summons, records |
| **11(4)** | Interim measures — suspend trading, restrain persons, impound proceeds, attach accounts |
| **11A** | Powers over issue of capital, transfer of securities, listing |
| **11AA** | Definition of a **collective investment scheme** |
| **11B** | Power to issue **directions**, including **disgorgement** |
| **11C** | **Investigation** — investigating authority, seizure of books |
| **11D** | Cease and desist proceedings |
| **12** | **Registration** of intermediaries |
| **12A** | Prohibition of manipulative and deceptive devices, insider trading, substantial acquisition |
| **15-I** | **Adjudicating Officer** — inquiry and penalty |
| **15J** | Factors for quantifying penalty — disproportionate gain, loss to investors, repetitive default |
| **15JB** | **Settlement (consent) of proceedings** |
| **15K–15M** | Establishment and composition of **SAT** |
| **15T** | **Appeal to SAT — within 45 days** |
| **15Z** | **Appeal to the Supreme Court on a question of law — within 60 days** |
| **24** | **Offences** — imprisonment up to 10 years and/or fine up to ₹25 crore |
| **26A–26E** | **Special Courts**, attachment and recovery, SEBI's first charge |
| **28A** | **Recovery** as arrears of land revenue |
| **30** | Power to **make regulations** |

## 2. Penalty sections (Chapter VI-A)

| Section | Default | Penalty as commonly cited |
|---|---|---|
| 15A | Failure to furnish information/returns | ₹1 lakh per day, up to ₹1 crore |
| 15B | Failure to redress investor grievances | ₹1 lakh/day, up to ₹1 crore |
| 15D | Defaults by a mutual fund | ₹1 lakh/day, up to ₹1 crore |
| 15E | AMC failing to observe investment norms | up to ₹1 crore |
| 15F | Broker defaults | up to ₹1 crore |
| **15G** | **Insider trading** | **₹10 lakh minimum**, up to **₹25 crore or 3× the profit, whichever is higher** |
| **15HA** | **Fraudulent and unfair trade practices** | **₹5 lakh minimum**, up to **₹25 crore or 3× the profit, whichever is higher** |
| 15HB | Residual penalty | ₹1 lakh minimum, up to ₹1 crore |

> ⚠ These amounts have been revised by amendment more than once (notably the Securities Laws (Amendment) Act, 2014 and the Finance Act, 2018). Confirm current figures from the **bare Act on sebi.gov.in** before the exam.

**Settlement (S.15JB):** proceedings may be settled on payment of a settlement amount under the **SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018**. Settlement is **without admission of guilt**, is discretionary, and cannot be claimed as a right.

## 3. Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)

- Established under **Section 15K**; principal bench at **Mumbai**.
- **Presiding Officer** — a sitting or retired Supreme Court judge or Chief Justice of a High Court — plus judicial and technical members.
- Hears appeals against orders of **SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA and IBBI** — a commonly missed fact.
- **45 days** to appeal from receipt of the order; delay condonable for sufficient cause.
- Not bound by the **CPC**; follows natural justice and regulates its own procedure.
- Further appeal: **Supreme Court on a question of law within 60 days** (extendable by 60 days).

## 4. How an enforcement case actually moves

```
Surveillance alert / complaint / reference
  -> Preliminary examination
  -> Investigation (S.11C)
  -> Show-cause notice
  -> Adjudication (S.15-I) OR Directions (S.11B / 11(4)) OR Prosecution (S.24, Special Court)
  -> Order: penalty, disgorgement, debarment, suspension or cancellation of registration
  -> Appeal: SAT (45 days) -> Supreme Court on a question of law (60 days)
```

A parallel route — **settlement under S.15JB** — is available at most stages.

---

## Exam pointers

1. **45 days** to SAT, **60 days** to the Supreme Court.
2. SAT hears **SEBI + IRDAI + PFRDA + IBBI** appeals.
3. **11B = directions · 11C = investigation · 15-I = adjudication · 15JB = settlement · 28A = recovery**.
4. Disgorgement is **not** a penalty — it removes unlawful gain and can be ordered *in addition to* a penalty.
5. S.24 offences: up to **10 years** imprisonment and/or ₹25 crore fine, tried by a **Special Court**.
