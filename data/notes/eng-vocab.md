# Vocabulary, Idioms and Word Usage

## How to build vocabulary efficiently

Do not memorise word lists alphabetically. Instead:
1. **Harvest words from your daily reading** — the editorial you already read for current affairs.
2. Record them as **word + the sentence you found it in + one synonym + one antonym**.
3. Put them in the **Flashcards** section of this app and let spaced repetition do the work.
4. Learn **roots and affixes** — they let you guess unfamiliar words in the exam.

## Register

For the descriptive paper, prefer **precise formal vocabulary over ornate vocabulary**. *Mitigate* is better than *ameliorate* if you are not certain of the nuance. A misused impressive word costs more than a correctly used ordinary one.

---

## Exam pointers

1. **Harvest words from reading**, not from alphabetical lists.
2. Learn **roots** — they let you attack unseen words.
3. *Disinterested* = impartial; *uninterested* = bored. This pair appears constantly.
4. *Comprise*, *flout/flaunt*, *prescribe/proscribe* are perennial favourites.
5. In descriptive writing, **precision beats decoration**.
